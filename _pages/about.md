Welcome to my page! My name is Dunyao Xue, a second-year PhD student at the [Institute of Statistics and Big Data](http://isbd.ruc.edu.cn/), Renmin University of China, advised by [Dr. Cheng Meng](https://cheng-bdal.github.io/) and [Dr. Wenlin Dai](https://sites.google.com/view/wenlindai/).

Prior to this, I received a BSc degree in Mathematics from the [Cuiying Honors School](https://cycollege.lzu.edu.cn/), Lanzhou University.

My current research focuses on `big data compression`, `optimal transport`, and `large language model architectures`. I care about methods that keep large systems compact, geometrically meaningful, and easier to reason about.
